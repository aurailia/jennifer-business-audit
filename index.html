The Gaps, Ranked by Cost

Resolved on our call

Your niche was the biggest gap. It's closed now.

Everything that used to say "sensitive, overthinking women who feel stuck" now has a real answer: women in long-term relationships who've lost themselves and can't get through a conversation without it going sideways. Every fix below exists to carry that one sentence through your entire business.

Gap 1 · The whole funnel runs through this

There's no low-ticket offer pulling people into a community

Right now, nobody who discovers you has a cheap, easy first purchase that also gets them somewhere ongoing. The audio series activation fixes this in one move: she buys it, and that purchase drops her straight into your free community. One product does two jobs.

Gap 2 · Where the real revenue lives

There's no community for the high-ticket sale to happen inside

Your free 60-minute session is currently the only path to paid work, and it happens with total strangers. In the model we mapped out, the sale happens differently: she's already inside your community, she already trusts you, and then the low-ticket paid session sells her into the signature container. That's a much warmer sale than anything happening on the website today.

Gap 3

The signature container isn't built or named

A 1-month or 3-month high-ticket offer is the destination of this whole path, and it doesn't exist yet, not named, not structured, not priced.

Gap 4

The site doesn't sound like you, and doesn't speak to her

The current Wix site feels templated rather than personal, and the copy is general enough to apply to almost any coach. Once the niche is the frame, the "Playful Coaching Session" CTA, the generic hero copy, and the missing personal photos and video all need a rebuild through that lens.

Gap 5

Podcast guesting has nowhere to send people

Your guesting strategy already works, three recordings have generated real invitations. But right now those appearances don't point anywhere specific. Once the community exists, every appearance gets one clear destination: come join the community, not a vague "check out my website."

Gap 6

Tools are scattered

Wix already has booking, courses, email marketing, and automation built in. There's no need for Calendly or a separate CRM once these are set up properly, but right now none of it is connected.

Nine fixes, in the order to build them. Each one is a link in one connected chain: a low-ticket offer that pulls people into a free community, a paid tier once it's proven itself, and a warm path from there into your signature high-ticket container. Every piece is built around the woman we defined together: in a long-term relationship, has lost herself, wants to be heard by her partner again.

1

Rebuild the site copy around her, specifically

Rewrite the homepage, the about section, and every call to action to speak directly to the woman in a long-term relationship who's lost herself and can't get through a conversation without it going sideways. Replace "Playful Coaching Session" with something that says plainly what she gets. Add real photos and video, including you and your husband, so the site finally carries your actual story instead of a template's. Add a simple Work With Me page that points to the audio series as the starting point, since that's now the front door into everything else.

Why it fits you: a specific woman with a specific ache is who refers you to a friend, a stage, or a podcast host. Nobody refers "someone who helps people who feel stuck."
2

Build the audio series activation, priced to sell

Package a focused, genuinely excellent audio series around the exact moment she's in: lost herself, can't get through a conversation, still loves him. This is the front door to your entire ecosystem, so it needs to feel like a real activation, not a throwaway freebie. Price it low enough to be an easy yes.

Why it fits you: this single purchase does two jobs at once. It's income that arrives without you on a call, and it's the ticket into the community, which is where the real relationship with her begins.
3

Build the free community on Wix, unlocked by the audio series

When she buys the audio series, she gets access to your free community. This is where podcast listeners get sent too, not to a generic website page. The free tier is the room everyone starts in.

Why it fits you: your 4th line runs on relationships and community, not solo grinding. This gives you one place to build real relationship with your audience instead of scattering it across Spotify, email, and a website.
4

Open the paid tier once you hit 50 members

At 50 free members, introduce a $33/month upgrade: monthly group coaching calls and a growing library of tools. Let the community's own needs guide what gets added to that library over time, rather than building it all up front.

Why it fits you: waiting for 50 members before adding the paid tier means you're not maintaining an empty room. It also matches how you're built to work, responding to what's actually needed rather than guessing in advance.
5

Sell the low-ticket session inside the community, into your signature container

Build the paid session, in the $44 to $57 range, as the offer you make to your warmest people, the ones already inside the community. From there it sells into your signature high-ticket container: a 1-month or 3-month offer. Name it, structure it, and price it now, since this is the destination the whole ecosystem is built to reach.

Why it fits you: selling from inside a warm community, not to a cold stranger on your website, is a completely different, much easier conversation. It also fits your Projector strategy: she's already invited you in by joining and engaging, so the offer lands as recognition, not a pitch.
6

Retire the free 60-minute session

Once the ecosystem above exists, the free hour no longer needs to be your front door. It's been quietly giving away your time to strangers and deterring the high-ticket clients you actually want. The audio series and the community now do that job better.

Why it fits you: your energy is your most limited resource. This protects it for the people who've already shown they're serious, instead of spending it on the first stranger who books a call.
7

Rebuild the quiz around the new niche

The existing quiz was built before the niche was defined, so it's being redone from the ground up rather than tweaked, questions, results pages, and printables rebuilt around the woman who's lost herself in a long-term relationship. Once it exists, it can feed directly into the audio series. This is something Claude can build directly with you.

Why it fits you: the time you already put into the original quiz wasn't wasted, the structure and format can carry over. It just needs to ask the right questions for the right woman now.
8

Decide the podcast direction

Decide whether to relaunch "Who Says" as your own show, now built around this niche, or continue guesting on shows like Stacy's. Either path works, but it should be decided once the niche is fully settled into your copy, and every appearance from here forward needs one clear call to action: come join the community.

Why it fits you: guesting is already generating organic invitations exactly as your Projector design predicts. The fix isn't the strategy, it's giving it somewhere specific to send people.
9

Build the email marketing engine inside Wix

Use Wix's native booking, course delivery, email marketing, and automation instead of a separate CRM or Calendly. This is a build Aurailia will help you put together directly: the audio series delivery, the community invitation, and the path from free member to paid tier, all wired through one system.

Why it fits you: fewer moving parts means less upkeep, and upkeep is exactly what pulls you out of your zone of genius and into daily grinding. You don't have to build this piece alone.
One thing not to fix: your tone. And one thing to add deliberately: the spiritual thread. You want to stop saying "getting unstuck" and start framing this season as an invitation from the universe. That language belongs in the brand voice everywhere above, not held back for later.

Build the Front Door and the Community

Month 1: Build the Audio Series + the Community Shell

  • Record and package the audio series activation around the woman who's lost herself in her marriage.
  • Set up the free community on Wix and connect it so a completed purchase unlocks access automatically.
  • Rewrite the homepage and core copy around the confirmed niche, and replace the "Playful Coaching Session" CTA.

Month 2: Wire the Funnel + Rebuild the Quiz

  • Build the Wix-native email sequence with Aurailia's help: audio series delivery, community invitation, and welcome.
  • Add a simple Work With Me page pointing to the audio series as the starting point.
  • Rebuild the quiz from the ground up around the new niche, using Claude to redo the questions, results, and printables.

Month 3: Send Every Path to the Community

  • Redirect podcast appearances to point listeners to the audio series and the community, not a generic website link.
  • Add real photos and video that carry your personal story, including you and your husband.
  • Decide the podcast direction: relaunch "Who Says" around the niche, or continue guesting, or both.
90-day milestone: a working audio series that unlocks a free community, a site that speaks directly to her, a rebuilt quiz, and every podcast appearance pointing to one clear destination.
MONTHS 4 TO 6

Grow the Room, Then Open the Paid Tier

Goal: 50 members, a $33/month upgrade with real value inside it, and your first sales into the signature container.

Month 4: Grow to 50

  • Focus visibility efforts on filling the free community: podcast guesting, warm outreach, and genuine participation in a few other relationship and personal growth spaces.
  • Show up inside the community on a sustainable rhythm, building real relationship with the members already there.
  • Name and structure your signature high-ticket container (1-month or 3-month) so it's ready the moment the paid tier opens.

Month 5: Open the Paid Tier

  • Launch the $33/month upgrade once you hit 50 members: monthly group coaching calls plus a starting tool library.
  • Build the low-ticket paid session ($44 to $57) as the offer for your warmest community members.
  • Let the tool library grow based on what members actually ask for, rather than building it all up front.

Month 6: Sell Into the Signature Container

  • Begin offering the low-ticket session to engaged community members, selling from there into your 1-month or 3-month container.
  • Review the numbers: audio series sales, free-to-paid tier conversion, session-to-container conversion.
  • Decide on the next collaboration phase with Aurailia, whether that's continued monthly strategy support or a deeper build.
6-month milestone: a community that grew itself from a low-ticket offer, a paid tier earning monthly recurring revenue, and a warm, proven path from your community straight into your signature high-ticket container.
